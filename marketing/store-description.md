# Store description

Minkowski Kart is a free, open-source 3D relativistic kart-racing game created by Robson Christie and built from SuperTuxKart. Lower the in-game speed of light, watch beta and gamma climb, and compare race time with your kart's proper-time clock while familiar arcade racing bends into something stranger.

Retarded-position and aberration effects reshape the view at speed. A conditional Doppler-inspired colour effect adds another visual cue. Physics-themed items include a radius-limited time-dilation field, a homing black-hole projectile with lensing, and linked wormholes that teleport racers between endpoints.

Play with a keyboard or gamepad, including local split-screen and LAN modes. Working experimental builds are supported for Windows, macOS, Linux and Android. Minkowski Kart's Bullet-based arcade dynamics use a speed cap and are not a complete special- or general-relativity simulation.
