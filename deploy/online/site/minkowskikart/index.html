How to Host & Play Online

1

Create an Account / Login

Launch MinkowskiKart, go to the Online menu option, and login with your username. Lobbies are authenticated against the database.

2

Configure Your Game

To host a room, select Global Networking, then click Create Server. Specify your server name, game rules, track, and bot count.

3

Publish and Play

Once you click Start Server, the client will register your server's AES handoff keys and public port. The room will automatically register and show up on this dashboard for other players to join!

4

Connecting via WAN

No complex port forwarding is required. ENet UDP matchmaking utilizes STUN (port 3478) to perform NAT punch-through so you can connect directly with other players globally.
