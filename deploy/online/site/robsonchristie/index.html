Research Shape

My work sits where quantum theory becomes noisy, numerical, and observable: environments, measurement records, rare events, and the early universe.

Open systems

Lindblad dynamics and stochastic trajectories

Analytical and numerical methods for Gaussian states, stochastic Schrodinger dynamics, quantum jumps, decoherence, and transition rates.

Cosmology

Quantum fields under gravitational coarse-graining

Lattice and stochastic-inflation calculations studying non-adiabatic excitation, environmental monitoring, tunnelling, and metastability.

Tools

Reproducible computation and simulation

Python, Matlab, Mathematica, QuTiP, Qiskit, HPC workflows, and research software for turning formal models into verifiable results.

Publications And Preprints

Short summaries from my CV, with direct links to papers, preprints, and project pages.

Quantum Stochastic Inflation

in preparation

Formulates stochastic inflation in an open-quantum-system framework, deriving GKLS dynamics for coarse-grained scalar field variables and recovering classical stochastic-inflation Fokker-Planck dynamics in the light-field regime.

Manuscript in preparation

Non-Equilibrium Dynamics of the Time-Dependent Excitonic Coupling in Fluorescent Protein Dimers

2026

Combines transition-density coupling calculations with stochastic quantum simulations to quantify strong near-field excitonic coupling in dimeric Venus fluorescent protein.

arXiv:2605.00027

Cosmic Lockdown: When Decoherence Saves the Universe from Tunneling

2025

Lattice simulations show how non-adiabatic dynamics can populate false vacua, while decoherence sourced by heavy modes suppresses later tunnelling and stabilises metastable states.

arXiv:2512.14204

Transition Path and Interface Sampling of Stochastic Schrodinger Dynamics

2025

Extends path and interface sampling to quantum trajectories, enabling efficient transition-rate estimation and revealing low-temperature non-Arrhenius behaviour linked to measurement backaction.

arXiv:2411.00490

Quantum Simulation of Dynamical Transition Rates in Open Quantum Systems

2024

Develops a quantum-simulation protocol for estimating open-system transition rates without full Lindblad evolution, decomposing rates into independently measurable contributions and validating on IBM quantum hardware.

arXiv:2412.17229

The Sound of Decoherence

2024

Maps density-matrix structure into sound so listeners can track tunnelling, thermalisation, decoherence, and recoherence through perceptual features.

arXiv:2412.17045 Sonification project

Quantum Tunnelling and Thermally Driven Transitions in a Double Well Potential at Finite Temperature

2024

Maps the thermal-to-quantum tunnelling crossover in a dissipative double well and introduces a Lindblad Brownian-motion model that removes the zero-temperature singularity.

Journal DOI arXiv:2312.12101

Quantum-jump vs stochastic Schrodinger dynamics for Gaussian states with quadratic Hamiltonians and linear Lindbladians

2022

Compares continuous and jump unravellings of Lindblad dynamics, deriving closed-form Gaussian evolution for continuous trajectories and a Gaussian Hagedorn-wavepacket scheme for jump trajectories.
