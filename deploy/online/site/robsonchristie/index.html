Open Quantum Systems, Universal Applications

I study quantum systems that interact with their environment. My work uses Lindblad dynamics, stochastic trajectories, decoherence theory, and numerical simulation across cosmology, quantum simulation, chemical physics, and quantum fields.

methods: GKLS/Lindblad equations | stochastic Schrodinger dynamics | quantum jumps | lattice simulation | Qiskit | QuTiP
