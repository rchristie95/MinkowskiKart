## Full Description (4000 chars max)

Race at the speed of light!

Minkowski Kart is a free, open-source arcade kart racer built on top of
SuperTuxKart — completely reimagined through the lens of special and general
relativity.

Every weapon, powerup, and track hazard has been rethemed to match the physics
of spacetime. Harness black holes, wormholes, photon beams, and time dilation
to demolish your opponents across 20+ tracks.

──────────────────────────────────────────────
🚀 POWERUPS FROM THE FUTURE
──────────────────────────────────────────────

• Warp Bubble — Deploy a relativistic shield or drop a trap on the track
• Black Hole — Launch a slow, homing singularity at the kart ahead of you
• Asteroid — Lob a dense, ballistic projectile that follows a gravity arc
• Photon — Fire a tethering beam forward, or hit everyone behind you with a
  Doppler flash
• Super Position — Collapse all track pickups in a world-pulse of quantum chaos
• Anti-Karticle — Release a mirror-clone of yourself that annihilates on contact
• Wormhole — Spawn two linked portals; any kart that enters one exits the other
• Time Dilation — Slow every other racer simultaneously
• Maxwell-Boltzmann — Plague the race leader with chaotic Brownian velocity kicks
• Zipper — Classic rocket boost, unchanged

──────────────────────────────────────────────
⚡ FEATURES
──────────────────────────────────────────────

• Free and open source (GPLv3)
• No ads, no in-app purchases, no tracking
• Full gamepad, keyboard, and touch support
• Local split-screen multiplayer
• AI opponents with multiple difficulty levels
• 20+ tracks, including all classic SuperTuxKart courses
• Custom relativistic screen effects: aberration, Doppler colour shift,
  spacetime grid distortions
• Works offline, no internet required to play
• Android TV / Leanback launcher support

──────────────────────────────────────────────
📚 EDUCATIONAL VALUE
──────────────────────────────────────────────

Each powerup is a playful, action-game interpretation of a real physics concept.
Wormholes, time dilation, anti-matter, black holes, the Maxwell-Boltzmann
distribution, and quantum superposition — experience them hands-on while racing.

──────────────────────────────────────────────
🔓 OPEN SOURCE
──────────────────────────────────────────────

Minkowski Kart is a fork of SuperTuxKart (supertuxkart.net), itself licensed
under GPLv3. All source code, assets, and build scripts are available on GitHub.
Contributions are welcome.
