## Notes

SuperTuxKart (the upstream game) is rated **Everyone / PEGI 3** on all
storefronts. Minkowski Kart makes no changes to content type — only to the
visual theme of the items (physics concepts replace cartoon objects). The
rating should be identical to SuperTuxKart.
