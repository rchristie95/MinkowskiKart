# Privacy Policy — Minkowski Kart: Relativistic Racing

**Last updated: May 2026**

## Overview

Minkowski Kart: Relativistic Racing ("the App") is a free, open-source arcade
kart racing game developed as a personal project and a fork of SuperTuxKart.

**We do not collect, store, transmit, or sell any personal data.**

---

## Data Collection

The App does **not** collect:

- Personal information (name, email, phone number, etc.)
- Location data
- Device identifiers or advertising IDs
- Usage analytics or telemetry
- Crash reports sent to any external server
- Photos, files, or any content from your device

---

## Internet Permission

The App requests the `INTERNET` permission solely to support the optional
in-game online multiplayer feature inherited from SuperTuxKart (STK online
servers). If you do not use online multiplayer, no network connection is ever
established.

No data is sent to any server operated by the developer of this fork.

---

## External Storage Permission

The App requests read/write access to external storage solely to:

1. Store user configuration files and saved replays in the standard Android
   game data directory.
2. Allow loading of custom user-provided add-on content.

No files are uploaded or shared with any third party.

---

## Third-Party Services

The App does **not** integrate any third-party SDKs, analytics platforms,
advertising networks, or social login services.

The App uses STK's optional online servers (operated by the SuperTuxKart
project, not by us) only when the user explicitly chooses to play online.
Please refer to the SuperTuxKart privacy policy at https://supertuxkart.net
for information about those servers.

---

## Children's Privacy

The App is suitable for all ages and does not collect any data from children
or anyone else.

---

## Changes to This Policy

If this privacy policy changes, the updated version will be committed to the
public source repository and the updated date above will be changed.

---

## Contact

If you have any questions about this privacy policy, please open an issue on
the public GitHub repository for this project.
