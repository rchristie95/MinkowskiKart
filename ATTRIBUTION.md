## OpenRelativity

The relativistic visual model and colour-shift work draw on the MIT Game Lab's [OpenRelativity](https://github.com/MITGameLab/OpenRelativity). Reused OpenRelativity code is provided under the MIT License and is identified in source comments and the technical manuscript under `paper/ajp-minkowski-kart/`.

## Assets

The repository contains artwork, audio, tracks, fonts, translations, and models from many contributors under multiple compatible licences. Their local `License.txt`, `licenses.txt`, `CREDITS`, and similar records under `data/` and `stk-assets/` are part of the distribution and take precedence for the assets they describe.

Do not copy an asset out of the project without first checking its adjacent attribution record. In particular, the marketing concept images under `store/graphics/` do not currently have a clear adjacent provenance record and should not be treated as gameplay captures.

The website uses genuine in-game captures from `paper/ajp-minkowski-kart/figures/`, retained under the project's distribution terms.
